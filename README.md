# Atliq-Sales-Analysis
Data Analysis with PowerBI


AtliQ Motors is an automotive giant from the USA specializing in electric vehicles(EV). In the last 5 years, their market share rose to 25% in electric and hybrid vehicles segment in North America. As a part of their expansion plans, they wanted  to launch their bestselling models in India where their market share is less than 2%. 
Bruce Haryali, the chief of AtliQ Motors India wants to do a detailed market study  of existing EV/Hybrid market in India before proceeding further.

Preliminary Research Questions:  

1. List the top 3 and bottom 3 makers for the fiscal years 2023 and 2024 in 
terms of the number of 2-wheelers sold. 
2. Identify the top 5 states with the highest penetration rate in 2-wheeler 
and 4-wheeler EV sales in FY 2024. 
3. List the states penetration rate in EV sales from 2021 
to 2024? 
4. What are the quarterly trends based on sales volume for the top 5 EV 
makers (4-wheelers) from 2022 to 2024? 
5. How do the EV sales and penetration rates in Delhi compare to 
Karnataka for 2024? 
6. What are the peak and low season months for EV sales based on the 
data from 2022 to 2024?
